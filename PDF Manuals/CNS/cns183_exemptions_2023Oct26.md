[[CNS]] EXEMPTIONS 
SEPTEMBER 13, 2022 
[[CNS]]® exemptions are elections provided by each [[Member]] that controls the delivery of all or part of any short 
position. This exemption instruction applies to any short positions the [[Member]] has on their [[CNS]] Projection 
Report: AutoRoute 02042022 file / AutoRoute 02040009 report, issued the day before the [[settlement]] date. By 
indicating a particular quantity as an exemption, the [[Member]] directs [[The Corporation]] not to settle certain short 
positions or portions thereof. Exemptions govern short positions in the [[CNS]] [[Stock]] Record and not [[DTC]] 
positions. All short positions or positions thereof for which no exemption is indicated are settled automatically to 
the extent that the [[Member]] has made such [[securities]] available in the [[Member]]'s [[DTC]] account, or they become 
available in its [[DTC]] account through other depository activity. 
[[Member]] Instructions 
Each [[Member]] has a standing exemption instruction for both subaccount A (General Account) and subaccount 
S ([[CNS]] ACATS Short Account) on the [[NSCC]] master file. They can be the same or different for each of these 
subaccounts. The [[Member]] informs [[NSCC]] of this instruction at the time they activate their account. This 
standing instruction can be updated by the [[Member]] at any time by submitting a request change with the [[NSCC]] 
account administration. A [[Member]] should work with their relationship manager if they decide to make a 
change.  
The following table shows the standing exemption instruction levels: 
Level Description 
Level 0 or Level 3 
- No Exemption 
None. All the [[Member]]’s short positions will be covered to the extent [[shares]] are available in their [[DTC]] 
account. For each [[short position]], the [[Member]] is electing [[CNS]] to have [[DTC]] deliver all free and 
available [[shares]] from its [[DTC]] account to [[CNS]] 888 account (or [[CNS]] 8902 account for [[CNS]] ACATS). 
The [[Member]]’s transactions display in the [[DTC]]’s ATP system. 
Level 1-Full 
Lockup 
The [[Member]] is instructing [[CNS]] to withhold part or all of a delivery from both the night and day cycle. 
This indicates the portion of the [[short position]] that should not be delivered. For short positions that 
have a Level 1 exemption, the [[Member]]’s transactions will not display in the [[DTC]]’s ATP system 
unless the [[Member]] submits a deliver order ([[DO]]) instruction directly to [[DTC]].

The [[Member]] is instructing [[CNS]] to withhold part or all of a delivery from both the night and day cycle 
except if a certain qualified activity occurs in their account at [[DTC]], such as a bank receipt, [[collateral]] 
loan release, or code deposit. When this qualified activity occurs that creates a position in the 
[[Member]]’s [[DTC]] account, the exemption is lifted or removed, and the [[CNS]] position becomes free to 
settle. For short positions that have a Level 2 exemption, the [[Member]]’s transactions will not display 
in the [[DTC]]’s ATP system unless the qualified activity occurs, or the [[Member]] submits a deliver order 
([[DO]]) instruction directly to [[DTC]]. 
Note: Level 2 Exemptions are only applicable for subaccount A (General Account). They are 
not applicable for subaccount S ([[CNS]] ACATS Short Account). 
 
For each [[settlement]] date, [[CNS]] uses these standing instructions unless a [[Member]] submits an Exemption 
Override instruction on the business day before [[settlement]]. Standing Exemption instructions govern all of the 
[[Member]]’s short positions for any day on which specific daily Exemption instructions: 
• Are not submitted to [[The Corporation]]. 
• Are not received by [[The Corporation]]. 
• Are unable to be processed by [[The Corporation]].

One Day Settling Exemption 
Members also have a One Day Settling (ODS) exemption level standing instruction on the [[NSCC]] master file. 
This ODS exemption must be the same for both the subaccount A (General Account) and subaccount S ([[CNS]] 
ACATS Short Account). This exemption is applied to a [[Member]]’s short positions created because of next day 
or same day settling transaction activity in their account received by [[CNS]] after the [[CNS]] Projection file/report 
was issued the business day before [[settlement]] date.1 The ODS standing exemption level instruction can only 
be Level 0 or 1 and cannot be overridden daily2. A [[Member]] who has a Level 1 instruction on a position and 
wants to satisfy a delivery to [[DTC]] must process a free deliver order to the 888 account (or [[CNS]] 8902 account 
for [[CNS]] ACATS) before the [[CNS]] cutoff time, which generally occurs daily at 3:10 P.M. Eastern Time. After this 
occurs, the [[Member]] can view the transactions in the [[DTC]] ATP system.
