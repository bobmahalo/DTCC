[[GSD]] processes several types of transactions including:
Buy/sell transactions of U.S. Government [[securities]],Dealer-to-dealer and [[Broker]]-to-dealer DVP Repos (including overnight, same-day settling, forward-starting and term
Repo trades),
Treasury Auction Takedown trades,
GCF Repo transactions,
CCIT Transactions, andSponsored GC transactions.
[[GSD]] offers the following types of membership:
Netting Membership,
Sponsored Membership,
CCIT Membership,Comparison-Only Membership, and
Funds-Only Settling Bank Membership.
[[GSD]]’s comparison service for Comparison-Only Members provides trade comparison services but does not provide trade
[[Novation]] and guarantee of [[settlement]], nor does it provide its netting, [[settlement]] or risk management services to Comparison-
Only Members.
Participating Members include:
Repo brokers,
Banks,
Dealers,
Government [[securities]] issuers,Foreign persons,
Registered clearing agencies, and
Qualified institutional buyers (QIBs) who meet [[GSD]]’s membership requirements.
