TRANSCRIPT OVERVIEW 
What follows is a transcript of the eLearning course that explains how to enter a trade (from a dealer’s point of 
view) in [[FICC]] MBSD RTTM® Web as it looks and functions following the “modernization” of the system in 2021. 
It is provided for reference only and the images may or may not accurately reflect the contents of the eLearning 
and current [[FICC]] MBSD RTTM® Web Production environment. The text below the slide images represents the 
information & navigation instructions included in the Notes panel of the eLearning player. This text and the 
annotations on the slides themselves should be taken together for education purposes. The slide numbers 
shown correlate to the slide numbers appearing in the course outline. These are included to help mitigate any 
confusion which may result from referencing page numbers in this transcript. We encourage you to review this 
and other courses in the “Using the New RTTM
® Web Interface” series available on the Learning Center and to 
complete the “Entering an MBSD Trade: Dealer Edition” online survey to provide us with your feedback.

1.16 PAR 
 
Notes: 
Steps (cont.): 
8. Enter a valid amount in the Par* field (*required).  
Here, we are entering “50MM.” Note that the system changed “50MM” to “50,000,000” when we clicked or 
tabbed out of this field.  
For anything over a million, you can enter the number of millions followed by “MM.” For thousands, follow 
the number with a single “M.”  
Click NEXT.

1.17 PRICE 
 
Notes: 
Steps (cont.): 
9. Enter the Price* (*required).  
The system accepts decimal numbers that are divisible by 1/512 (up to 3 places before the decimal point 
and up to 9 places after the decimal point). 
We’ll set it at “99.375” for this trade.  
Click NEXT.
