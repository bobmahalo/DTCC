TRANSCRIPT OVERVIEW 
What follows is a transcript of the eLearning course that explains how to use RTTM® to enter a trade (from the 
[[Broker]] point of view). It is provided for reference only and the images may or may not accurately reflect the 
contents of the eLearning and current [[FICC]] MBSD RTTM® Web Production environment. The text below the 
slide images represents the information & navigation instructions included in the Notes panel of the eLearning 
player. This text and the annotations on the slides themselves should be taken together for education 
purposes. The slide numbers shown correlate to the slide numbers appearing in the course outline. These are 
included to help mitigate any confusion which may result from referencing page numbers in this transcript. We 
encourage you to review this and other courses in the “Using the New RTTM® Web Interface” series available 
on the Learning Center and to complete the “Entering a Trade in [[FICC]] MBSD RTTM® Web: [[Broker]] Edition” 
online survey to provide us with your feedback.
