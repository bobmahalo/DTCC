FUND/SERV® REJECT CODES 
The following Fund/SERV® reject codes are used to indicate the reasons for rejecting a firm or fund’s input to 
the Fund/SERV® system.  They may also be used by funds when rejecting a firm or TPA’s input.   
In addition, these reject codes are used for these other [[NSCC]] products: ACATS-Fund/SERV, and Transfer of 
Retirement Assets (ToRA).
