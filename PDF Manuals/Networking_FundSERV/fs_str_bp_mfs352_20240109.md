INTRODUCTION 
A Short-Term Redemption fee (STR) is a charge to an [[investor]] who redeems mutual fund [[shares]] before a certain 
amount of time or holding period has elapsed. S hort-Term Redemptions are retained by and become assets of 
the Fund and are therefore included in the calculation of the daily net [[Asset]] value of the Fund.  
Prior to the introduction of this functionality, the handling of STRs was manual, inefficient, or non- standardized. 
This functionality makes processing and [[settlement]] of STR fees more efficient on Fund/SERV®. Th is 
functionality creates an automated process that enables firms to return STR proceeds to funds at the time which 
they are incurred, i.e., at the time of redemption. The automation and standardization benefits firms and funds, 
and mutual fund shareholders who would have assets returned more quickly to the funds in which they have 
invested. 
Definitions 
Omnibus Account :  Generally, an account whose position balance consists of [[shares]] from one or more 
underlying shareholders. One or more firms maintain the additional records of the underlying shareholders. 
Funds rely upon firms to calculate and maintain the share lot aging of the underlying shareholders.  
Non-Omnibus Account:  Generally, an account representing a single shareholder. Funds calculate and 
maintain the share lot aging on their respective [[transfer agent]] system. 
Processing Background 
On certain mutual fund accounts, such as omnibus accounts, firms are responsible for identifying redemptions 
that are subject to a short-term redemption fee, as well as the specific share lots and the    rate to be applied. 
Firms must calculate the amount of the STR and remit it back to the fund. Prior to the implementation of this 
functionality, STRs could not be processed simultaneously with the redemption on Fund/SERV®. Fees that 
applied to redemptions had to be settled separately between  firms and funds via a cash adjustment or a manual 
wire outside of Fund/SERV®. 
Alternative methods are available, both on and outside of Fund/SERV®, but they are not standardized, efficient, 
nor timely. Clients  are subject to increased wire expenses, additional manual processing, delayed trade 
confirmations, and potentially a greater  exposure to risk using these methods. The STR functionality helps to 
minimize or eliminate these issues and provide standardization among participants.  
Note: 
There are alternative methods for processing short-term redemption fees on networked non-omnibus accounts on 
Fund/SERV®. However, the confirmation data used in these methods mixes together all information into existing fields, 
without specifically referencing STRs. The STR functionality does not change these processes, except for the use of the 
redemption fee waiver indicator.

Redemption Fee Indicator 
Fund/SERV® redemptions that are subject to short-term redemption fees need to be readily identified. A 
"Redemption Fee Indicator" field may be used with the values of "0" = Short-Term Redemption Fee and "1" = 
STR Fee with CDSC. This indicator may be provided by the firm on the Order, As-Of Order, Correction, 
Exchange, and related record types. The indicator may also be used by the fund on the Confirmation, 
Correction Confirmation, Exchange Confirmation and related record types to respond to the firm.  
Funds are not allowed to submit this field or any of the STR fields described below to initiate the STR 
functionality when the fields have NOT been provided by the firm. This restriction is enforced through edit 
checks on Fund/SERV®.  
The indicator signals the fund to override or alter the process in which they calculate a short -term redemption 
fee for a transaction. The indicator prompts the fund to use the STR information provided on the transaction to 
help calculate the STR. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The Redemption Fee Indicator is located in the Order, As-Of Order, Correction, Exchange, and related record 
types.

Redemption Fee Waiver Indicator 
The optional Redemption Fee Waiver Indicator may be used on Fund/SERV® so that STRs may be waived in 
the same manner as CDSCs. This indicator may be used for all types of accounts ( i.e., not only omnibus 
accounts) and provides the reasons for the firm requesting the fee to be waived. As a best practice, this 
indicator should not be used in combination with the STR fields described in this guide.  
This indicator may be used by the firm on the Order, As-of Order, Correction, and Exchange. It may also be 
used by the fund on the Confirmation, Correction Confirmation, Exchange Confirmation and related record 
types to respond to the firm. Current descriptions of fee waivers can be found in Fund/SERV® record layouts in 
the documentation section of the learning center - see 'Short Term Redemption Fee Waiver Indicator fields. 
STR Fee Processing – STR Related Fields 
Redemption fee-related fields with STR indicators on Fund/SERV® may be used so that firms may provide 
information regarding the short-term redemption fee. The information allows the fund to determine the amount 
of STR that needs to be retained from the redemption proceeds. The descriptions below provide the definitions 
of the redemption fields and their usage on Fund/SERV® as they relate to STR. 
Redemption Fee Rate 
Effective September 26, 2016, “STR Fee Rate” field was renamed to “Redemption Fee Rate” (see Important 
Notice a8159). 
When a “Redemption Fee Indicator” of 1 or 2 is entered this field provides the rate of the short -term redemption 
fee and should be applied to the STR share quantity. This field is optional/conditional and should be provided 
to calculate the STR money amount when applicable. 
Only valid STR rates should be provided. Blended rates should not be used, and instead, separate 
transactions with different rates should be submitted. Another acceptable rate is 99.99999, which is considered 
to be equal to 100% and to be used for "fee-only" redemptions.

STR Fee Best Practices 
STR Related Fields and Functionality 
Unless otherwise noted, the fields and functionality should only be used in situations where the firm is providing 
the share lot aging calculations (e.g., omnibus account) and where the order includes an STR.  
The STR indicators should not be used for redemptions that are not subject to an STR, or when the STR is being 
waived (as indicated by the Redemption Fee Waiver Indicator). 
Fund [[Companies]] should use this functionality only to respond to a firm. Funds should not initiate this functionality 
when the firm has not provided the redemption fee indicator and other fields.  
Redemption Fee Indicator 
If Redemption Fee Indicator 1 or 2 is provided on a security that has no STR, then the indicator is meaningless 
and can be ignored. Funds may ignore and confirm the trade, soft-reject, or hard reject the order. 
Best Practice Recommendation: Since the security does not have an STR, it is recommended that the indicator 
be ignored, and the trade be confirmed. 
If a “Redemption Fee Indicator” is provided but is not applicable to the account, then funds must respond (i.e., 
confirm, soft-reject, or hard-reject) at their own discretion. This will be a fund-by-fund rule. 
Short Term Redemption Fee Waiver Indicator 
If a Short Term Redemption Fee Waiver Indicator is provided but is not applicable to the security type, then the 
indicator can be ignored. Funds may ignore and confirm the trade, soft-reject, or hard-reject the order. 
Best Practice Recommendation : Since the security does not have an STR fee, it is recommended that the 
indicator be ignored, and the trade be confirmed. 
If a Short Term Redemption Fee Waiver Indicator is provided but is not applicable to the account, then funds 
must respond (i.e., confirm, soft-reject, or hard-reject) at their own discretion. This will be a fund-by-fund rule. 
Redemption Fee Rate 
Best Practice Recommendation : If Redemption Fee Rate is not supplied, and STR Fee Share Quantity is 
supplied, then the fund should apply its standard [[default]] rate to the share quantity for calculating the fee. If a 
Redemption Fee Rate and an STR Fee Share Quantity were provided, then that rate provided by the firm would 
be used by the fund to calculate the fee. 
If the rate provided is not an applicable rate (i.e., a valid STR rate or one of the valid STR rates for funds that 
have multiple STR rates) then the order should be hard rejected. 
For the purpose of a "fee-only" redemption (see Scenario 4), the firms may request a 100% rate. An STR Fee rate 
of 99.99999 will be considered to be 100%. However, the best practice for a fee-only redemption is to use the 
“Redemption Fee Money Amount" field.

Redemption Fee Money Amount 
Best Practice Recommendation: The Redemption Fee Money Amount should be provided only in the 
following situations: 
• A specific STR amount is to be withheld. (see Scenario 3) 
• An order is transmitted representing only the amount of an STR to be applied to the referenced account 
and to be remitted by the firm. (see Scenario 4) 
 
If the amount in the “Redemption Fee Money Amount” field exceeds the money Amount of the trade, then the 
order should be hard rejected. 
For the purpose of a "fee-only" redemption (see Scenario 4), the firms should use this field to provide t he 
amount of the STR.

NETWORKING ACTIVITY FILE (F55) 
Funds use the Networking Activity file to send detailed information on short -term redemption fees associated 
with a direct redemption. Information regarding a direct redemption may be passed from the fund to the firm 
using the activity record F55. 
Direct redemptions marked as transaction type 02 may be more specifically described using the STR -related 
Networking Transaction Description codes of "BH - Short Term Redemption Fee" or "BI - STR with CDSC" as 
listed on the “Networking Transaction Description Codes” document found in the Mutual Fund Services  section 
of the Learning Center under " Codes" https://dtcclearning.com/products-and-services/mutual-fund-
services/codes.html 
Note: 
The transaction description codes will automatically acquire all related functionality and system validation inherent in using 
the transaction description codes. For example, [[settlement]] of the direct redemption and the short-term-redemption fee may 
occur through Networking when certain fields are set accordingly. 
Single F55 transaction to report direct redemptions and exchanges 
Prior to September 26, 2016, funds reporting a Direct Redemption with an STR (or STR with CDSC) fee provided 
two F55 Activity transactions. 
• The 1st Activity transaction indicated “Transaction Type” = “02-Direct Redemption” and reported a 
redemption dollar amount 
• The 2nd Activity transaction had “Transaction Type” = BH (STR Fee) or BI (STR with CDSC) and the STR 
fee amount in the “Dollar Amount”. 
 
Effective September 26, 2016, funds are no longer required to send two F55 Activity transactions when 
reporting a direct redemption (or an exchange) with a Redemption Fee. The following best practice provides 
guidance on reporting direct redemptions (or exchanges) with STR Fees:  
• In order to communicate a redemption fee (Short Term Redemption Fee) on the Activity Record funds 
should send a single F55 Activity transaction 
• Redemption Amount (netted of the fee) should be populated in the “Dollar Amount” field  
• “Redemption Fee Money Amount” field should contain the STR Fee Amount  
• “Redemption Fee Rate” should contain the STR Fee Rate 
• CDSC fee amount (if also applies) should be provided in the “Commission /CDSC Amount” field  
• “Transaction Type” field should be populated as follows: 
 
Direct Activity at the Fund Transaction Description Types 
Direct Redemption with an STR Fee BH 
Direct Redemption with an STR Fee with CDSC BI
