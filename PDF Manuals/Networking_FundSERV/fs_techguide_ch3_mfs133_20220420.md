Membership Process 7 
MEMBERSHIP PROCESS 
Purpose 
Firms and Funds have different requirements, but both must be members of [[NSCC]] in order to participate in 
Fund/SERV. 
Clearing Fund 
Unlike Funds, Firms must establish a clearing fund with [[NSCC]]. The amount of the Clearing Fund is determined 
based on the services the Firm wishes to participate in, including a Fund/SERV Only Firm category.  
Process 
Outlined below are the stages necessary to become a new mutual fund client. 
Stage Description 
1 Wealth Management Services will guide perspective client’s through the membership process.  Please contact 
them at 212-855-8877 or send an email to WMSconnect@[[DTCC]].com to get started.  
2 Questionnaire and Financial Documentation to [[NSCC]]: 
Potential members will submit their financial records and completed Membership questionnaires via a 
membership portal received in a link via email from [[DTCC]]’s Account Administration Department. The specific 
financial documents required would be dependent upon the applicant, e.g., bank, [[Broker]]/dealer, insurance 
company, etc. 
3 Review and Agreements to Applicant: 
Prospective client will be reviewed by Account Administration, Risk Analysis/Credit Review and in 
certain circumstances may require Board approval, to determine membership approval.   
4 [[NSCC]]  Account Administration will send the Firm or Fund an appropriate membership package to complete and  
return. These documents include, but are not limited to the following documents: 
• [[Member]] Agreements specific to your membership classification 
• Money [[settlement]] Fact Sheet (Back up wire Instructions), 
• Settling Bank Agreement 
Execution of Agreements: 
Signed Membership Agreements must be returned to [[NSCC]] accompanied by an opinion of outside counsel       and 
certificate of incumbency. [[NSCC]] will also provide and require that new members enter into a settling bank 
agreement to facilitate the daily net [[settlement]] with [[NSCC]]. 
5 Approval: 
Prospective members  will be notified of their approval. Other steps may proceed prior to  the approval, with the 
exception of account activation.

Fund/SERV [[Member]] Profile 9 
FUND/SERV [[Member]] PROFILE 
Purpose 
 
Every [[Member]] of Fund/SERV is required to complete a Fund/SERV [[Member]] Profile form. This enables [[NSCC]] 
to customize a [[Member]]’s participation in the system. Using the form, a client can select how they wish  to 
receive and send information. Other choices allow [[NSCC]] to restrict certain transactions on the  [[Member]]’s 
behalf. 
To access these forms, please contact your firm’s Super Access Coordinator (SAC) and have the SAC grant  
you access to the ‘MFS forms’ role under the Fund/SERV product. 
If a SAC does not have [[Entitlement]] to grant access to the Fund/SERV product, or if a SAC needs to be 
established, please contact Wealth Management Services at 212-855-8877. 
Note:  
There are ADD, MODIFY and DELETE forms that are used to communicate to [[NSCC]] any additions, deletions, or 
modifications to the membership information. 
Components of the [[Member]] Profile 
The [[Member]] Profile identifies the client and provides both generic information and processing capabilities  for 
the firm and Fund. The generic information includes: 
• [[Member]] name 
• Clearing number 
• Effective date 
 
Processing capabilities allows the [[Member]] to select processing requirements and allowable functions in the  
Fund/SERV system. These options include: 
• Firm exits 
• Cash adjustments 
• Exchange privileges 
• Output Distribution

Processing Cut-Off 
Times 
Any security designated as a Money Market Fund (Security Type = 24) must specify a cut-off 
time for their purchases and for their redemptions. These cut-off times are used by [[NSCC]] to 
either include or exclude transactions from that day’s [[settlement]] file. The times given should 
be Eastern Time (ET). This is the time [[NSCC]] must receive the order for that day’s 
processing. There is a separate time for purchase orders and redemptions orders.

If a Money Market security is coded with an 11:00AM deadline and a purchase is sent to 
Fund/SERV by 11:00AM, [[NSCC]] will process the trade and include it in that day’s [[settlement]] 
file. If the purchase order was sent to Fund/SERV after 11:00AM, it will not be included in that 
day’s [[settlement]] file.

Required Testing for New mutual fund members 19 
REQUIRED TESTING FOR NEW MUTUAL FUND 
MEMBERS 
Testing Options 
Fund/SERV is designed to offer two options for applications Testing. 
The first [[Option]] is to use the Test Indicator in the Fund/SERV Application  in Production. Even though 
Fund/SERV runs in [[DTCC]]’s production environment, a firm can select a Test Indicator of “1” to distinguish it as 
a “test" order. The order flow proceeds to completion, but no money obligation is generated. All reports and 
files, including money [[settlement]], are produced as if it were a real order being processed. If a firm selects a 
Test Indicator of “1” (1 = Test), no [[settlement]] records will be created or go out.  
The second choice is to use the test environment (PSE) for Fund/SERV. This is more appropriate for existing 
clients who must keep their production orders separate from their test orders.  
Example:  
Test edit cycles run less frequently than production. The production region has edit cycles throughout the day. 
Phases of Testing 
Fund/SERV testing has two separate and distinct phases. 
The first phase involves the client sending a test file to [[DTCC]]. This takes place after the client has been 
approved for membership and has satisfied our communication’s tests. 
The second phase involves the client moving to either the production or test environment.  
First Phase Testing 
In the first phase of testing, files are sent to [[DTCC]] where Connectivity checks to ensure the client is able to 
“connect” to [[DTCC]].  
The purpose of this test is to ensure the readiness of a firm. [[NSCC]] must be sure that new clients [[DO]] not pose a 
risk to other clients or to the Fund/SERV system. In turn, [[NSCC]] needs to make sure their files [[DO]] not 
jeopardize the client’s system. 
Monitoring By [[DTCC]] 
[[DTCC]] staff will monitor the testing process. Clearing Operations is also responsible for interacting with the 
clients submitting and receiving test data.  
DataTrak uses the SYSID to route files to their proper application, in this case, Fund/SERV. Clients will supply 
[[DTCC]]’s AutoRoute department with the proper job names needed for us to route our files correctly to them.

The same scenario takes place on the output side. Both the [[DTCC]]’s Integration Team will authorize set up of 
appropriate Autoroute files.  Again, the AutoRoute file numbers will be different if they come from the test 
region as opposed to the production region. 
How to Begin the Testing Process 
Coordination begins with the client contacting their Relationship Manager who will connect the client to all 
appropriate [[DTCC]] areas to coordinate testing.   
Once the client has successfully tested with [[DTCC]], production files will be set up.  
Test Partners 
Whether a client chooses to use the production or test environment, they must have a partner to test with. 
[[DTCC]] cannot act as the contra-side of the order process. 
[[NSCC]] recommends that test partners develop test scripts to follow.
