Introduction to Forms 5 
 INTRODUCTION TO FORMS 
Fund/SERV 
Fund/SERV [[Member]] and Security Issue [[ID]] forms allow funds to add, modify and delete Fund/SERV® 
[[securities]] as well as modify [[Member]] processing parameters on Fund/SERV® and Networking.   
These forms are on the MyDTCC portal under the Fund/SERV product menu [[Option]] - https://portal.[[DTCC]].com. 
To access these forms, you should contact your firm’s Super Access Coordinator (SAC) and have the SAC 
grant you access to the new ‘MFS forms’ role under the Fund/SERV product. Once the MFS forms role is 
granted, no further action is required. 
If a SAC does not have [[Entitlement]] to grant access to the Fund/SERV product, or if a SAC needs to be 
established, you may contact Wealth Management Services at 212-855-8877 to request access. 
Trust Fund/SERV Agreement –This is the standard Investment Company Institute (ICI) agreement 
regarding reporting responsibilities between the fund and the firm to exchange Fund/SERV information. This is 
a bilateral agreement to be signed by the fund and firm and is maintained by each respectively.  Please contact 
your Relationship or Wealth Management Services at 212-855-8877 to obtain this form. 
ToRA Agreement – This is the standard Investment Company Institute (ICI) agreement regarding 
processing responsibilities.  
Please contact your Relationship or Wealth Management Services at 212-855-8877 to obtain this form and 
additional information regarding ToRA. 
 
ACATS-Fund/SERV 
ACATS-[[default]] Registration Profile, ACATS-Fund/SERV Test [[Member]] Profile and ACATS-Fund/SERV Test 
Security [[ID]] Forms allow clients to choose specific defaults as well as test in ACATS-Fund/SERV test 
environment. 
Please contact your Relationship or Wealth Management Services at 212-855-8877 to obtain these forms and 
additional information regarding ACATS-Fund/SERV. 
 
Networking 
Networking Agreement –This is the standard Investment Company Institute (ICI) agreement regarding 
reporting responsibilities between the fund and the firm to exchange Networking information.  
Trust Networking Agreement –This is the standard Investment Company Institute (ICI) agreement regarding 
reporting responsibilities between the fund and the firm to exchange Networking information.  
Please contact your Relationship or Wealth Management Services at 212-855-8877 to obtain these forms and 
additional information regarding Networking.
