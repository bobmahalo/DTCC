Steps [[DTCC]]’s Task [[participant]]’s Task 
Step 3 – Physical Conversion 
[[DTC]] will work with the [[participant]] to schedule the movement of the physical 
inventory from the [[participant]]'s location or its current custodian. The 
conversion plan should take the following into account:  
Will the conversion be done as part of the regular work week or other (phased 
approach).       
 
• Daily Volume/ Order of Conversion: [[DTC]] and the [[participant]] will need to 
discuss how the [[participant]] wants to move the assets to [[DTC]] [[custody]]. The 
[[participant]] may wish to convert by box (inactive boxes first), or if doable, by 
""product"" (restricted inventory, etc.).
