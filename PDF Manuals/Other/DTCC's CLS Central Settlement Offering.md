Regulatory reform has led to a changing [[settlement]] landscape with a heightened focus on 
pre- and post-trade transparency, as well as an increased requirement for transactional 
reporting. 
CLS’ [[OTC]] [[Derivatives]] is an enhanced [[settlement]] 
service model for instructions relating to bilateral net 
payment [[obligations]] for the [[OTC]] credit [[Derivatives]] 
market. Created in partnership with The Depository 
Trust & Clearing Corporation ([[DTCC]]), the service links 
[[DTCC]]’s Trade Information Warehouse with CLS’ 
central multicurrency cash [[settlement]] service and 
automates the processing of [[OTC]] credit [[Derivatives]]-
related cash flows. 
 
The benefits 
• Fully automated processing to improve post-trade 
efficiency for [[Derivatives]] instruments (CDS, CDX, CDT) 
and greatly reduce operational risk 
 
• Optimize your operations – via straight-through 
processing and real-time information regarding 
[[settlement]] and confirmation 
 
• Drive funding efficiency and lower your [[Liquidity]] 
requirement through the use of multilateral netting

How it works 
• Supports CLS [[settlement]] members and 
their third-party clients to settle net 
payments in nine currencies: AUD, GBP, 
CAD, CHF, EUR, HKD JPY, SGD, and USD 
 
• Instruction Submissions: For each 
participating CLS [[settlement]] [[Member]], 
[[DTCC]] submits netted cash flow amounts 
for the next value date to CLS in the 
relevant currencies. 
 
• Validation: CLS validates and accepts 
the net payment instructions, which are 
then incorporated into each CLS 
[[Member]]'s pay-in schedule. 
The netted payments are then scheduled 
to settle on the next available business 
day, along with their submitted FX 
payment instructions.  
These cash flows and netted payments 
can be seen in the TIW and CLS GUIs. 
 
• Notification: Notifications are sent 
back to [[DTCC]] following [[settlement]] on 
value date.
