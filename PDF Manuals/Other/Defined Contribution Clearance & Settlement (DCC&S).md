Client roles are determined by the following trading arrangements:
Dual-Party Trading - This trading
arrangement assumes that a bank/trust
company, [[Broker]]/dealer, or insurance
company, acting as record keeper for
the plan, submits Fund/SERV®
transactions. Submission of these
transactions obligates the submitting
party for [[settlement]].
  
Tri-Party Trading - This trading
arrangement assumes that an [[NSCC]]
TPA [[Member]], acting as record keeper
for the plan, submits Fund/SERV®
transactions. Submission of these
transactions obligates the bank/trust
company, insurance company, or a
[[Broker]]/dealer for [[settlement]].
