Roles 
CRS has three levels of users: Super Access Coordinators (Super ACs), Access Coordinators (ACs), and  
Operators. Operators are end users of [[DTCC]]’s products. ACs are responsible for managing Operators.  
Super ACs are responsible for managing Operators, ACs, and other Super ACs.  
 
SACs can grant two types of roles when providing access to additional users: 
 Admin (Read/Write) access allows users full functionality to log in and add, remove, or edit their 
existing permissions. Once granted, Admin access enables users to make any necessary 
amendment to existing setups. [[DTCC]] generates full Activity Logs of all amendments made to user 
setups and all permission updates available withing the application. 
 Admin Read Only access allows users to log in and view their existing setup without having the 
ability to make any changes. 
 
ACCESSING MANAGER SERVICES / USER ROLES 
Super Access Coordinators (“SACs”) can grant “Manage Services” access to authorized onboarding users 
from their firm. 
Identifying Your SACs 
Use the “Need access to another product/service? Want to know who your administrators are?” link on your 
portal homepage to find your SACs. 
 
 
SACs Granting Operator Access 
[[DTCC]] operates under self-service model, wherein 2 SACs are nominated for each firm. These SACs can 
make any changes to user access/permission, with one submitting a request and the other approving the 
said request. 
Providing users with DDL PSE SAC will allow the user to grant permissions for DDL PSE, however 
they will not have access to the application. 
Users must also have Operator access (even if they are a SAC) to access PSE DDL.

FAQs 
 
 What are the changes on the new platform? 
[[Derivatives]] Distributed [[Ledger]] (DDL) is a new platform that is going to replace the existing TIW 
(Trade Information Warehouse).  
[[DTCC]]’s TIW and IHS Markit’s DSMatch share a systems architecture. Under this effort, we are 
separating the platforms in a highly coordinated migration as both firms upgrade their respective 
technologies. 
IHS Markit is replacing its DSMatch offering with their new platform, [[Tradeserv]]. As the industry’s 
primary confirmation system for the CDS market, [[Tradeserv]] will continue to be a key source of 
transaction data into [[DTCC]]’s TIW. 
 
 Will there be two applications for [[Tradeserv]] and TIW-DDL? 
Yes, firms need to access different applications for [[Tradeserv]] and TIW-DDL. 
For TIW DDL: https://ddlweb.ddl.apppse.[[DTCC]].org/ddlgui/gui/#/authentication 
 
 Will SACs, ACs and Operators in [[DTCC]] CRS System automatically get an access to DDL 
System? 
Yes, all the current SACs, ACs and Operators in [[DTCC]] CRS will be migrated to DDL. Should you 
wish to setup a new access request in DDL for your colleague in the future, you may refer to the 
CRS User Guide – DDL PSE file. 
 
 How will I know the SACs nominated on my firm? 
You may refer to the “Need access to another product/service? Want to know who your 
administrators are?” link on your [[DTCC]] portal homepage. 
 
 If there are no SACs nominated on my firm, to whom and how can we setup this request? 
[[DTCC]] will be collecting SAC forms from the client and setting this up with our Global Entitlements 
Team. 
 
 How to remove Operator access 
[[DTCC]] operates under self-service model, wherein 2 SACs are nominated for each firm. These 
SACs can make any changes to user access/permission, with one submitting a request and the 
other approving the said request. 
 
 
Should you have further questions, you may reach out to tiwsupport@[[DTCC]].com or call +1-212-855-1200.
