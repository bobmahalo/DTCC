• Go-live Preparation through January 27
 Prepare to stop submitting messages in current format beyond January 26 11:30 pm EST on go-live date.
 Prepare to stop submitting any messages between 12:00 am EST and 4:00 pm EST on January 27 .
• Go-live January 27
 For support after go-live, clients should reach out to Client Services (GTRSupport@[[DTCC]].com).
 Goal is for Firms to submit [[CFTC]], [[SEC]] and CANADA reportable messages starting at 4:00pm EST on Saturday January 27, 
subject to 2pm status email (including manual upload thru NoA Portal).
 Any delays to this timeframe will be communicated to the GTR North America Working Group via email. If you would like to add 
additional recipients to these communications, please email Stan Preston (spreston@[[DTCC]].com) to be included.
 [[participant]] reports in Re-Write Phase 2 format will be available in NoA Portal and through SFTP for the Report Date January 27.
• PPD Public Dashboard after Go-live 
 Please note all pending disseminations processed prior to Go-Live will be disseminated in current dissemination format.
 Dissemination for all PPD files in current production format. This includes any pending disseminations mentioned above. 
messages received between January 26, 12:00 AM EST until the application is stopped for Go-Live on January 26 11:30 PM will 
be included in the January 26 Cumulative Reports. 
 All successfully processed disseminations received from the time the application is stopped for Go-Live on January 27 will be 
published on January 28 in the January 27 Cumulative Reports.
