TIW’s Re-PlatformingThe re-platforming of [[DTCC]]’s Trade Information Warehouse (TIW) is nearing completion. We have been workingalongside OSTTRA (the new home of MarkitServ) as they reconfigure their confirmation system, [[Tradeserv]] – theindustry’s primary confirmation system for the CDS market – to leverage cloud and distributed [[Ledger]] technology, and weanticipate the enhanced platforms will go live in August 2022.The core functionality will largely remain the same as the existing DSMatch / TIW platform, however the UI has beenredesigned and the Onboarding process is changing.Funds will now be onboarded to [[Tradeserv]] and TIW respectively. The following slides outline the key responsibilities ofOSTTRA (Formerly known as IHS Markit) and [[DTCC]].

High Level Summary of ChangesOnboarding Process
•Today, OSTTRA’s DSMatch Onboarding team performs all account onboarding tasks for DSMatch and TIW•DSMatch, NCM and TIW services are accessed through the same Graphical User Interface (GUI) and [[DTCC]] MQ gateway. Transaction and MIS Reports are accessed via [[DTCC]] FTP applicationsCurrent BehaviourTarget Behaviour
•With the separation of technology, there is a new requirement to onboard with both [[Tradeserv]] (OSTTRA) and TIW ([[DTCC]]) platforms•Both OSTTRA and TIW Onboarding teams will work together to process onboarding requests•Account reconciliation to ensure both platforms are aligned•Payments, [[settlement]], Calc Flag, Adherence and Triggering of TIW services will continue to be accessed using the existing [[DTCC]] MQ gateway, FTP applications and new GUI.•All [[Tradeserv]] services will be accessed using the OSTTRA (or IHSMarkit) MQ gateway and new GUI and FTP applications.
