Agents to look up individual [[CUSIP]] details.

Working with the Account Overview Page 
From the Account Overview you can see any unconfirmed balances requiring your attention. If balances 
remain unconfirmed, they are color coded as they age to call attention to the need for action. For example , 
Green signifies today’s balance, yellow is two days from the report date, and red is 3 or more days from the 
report date. 
Balance Confirmations 
Agents are required to verify and confirm their balances for each Report date. This includes pending balances 
that have aged. Agents must confirm in order of oldest pending balances.  
 
Before Balance Confirmation – Any Balances Awaiting Confirmation Are Shown Oldest to Newest 
 (Red to Yellow to Green)

What Happens After Logging a Discrepancy? 
After you have logged a discrepancy, it will be researched by [[DTC]] Operations staff until both [[DTC]] and you as 
Agent are able to understand and document the cause of the difference. 
 
Account Overview Page with Discrepancy Logged 
You will also see any discrepancies logged against the balance on the Balance Confirmation pop-up modal 
window. These are on display to remind you to review and resolve any discrepancies prior to confirming the 
balance.

[[CUSIP]] List Page 10 
 
The [[CUSIP]] list includes a search allowing you to narrow the results. To open the [[CUSIP]] s earch, click the 
Refine Search link. 
 
You may also view details for each [[CUSIP]] in the list. To see a detail, click a [[CUSIP]] number  from the column 
on the left. 
 
The [[CUSIP]] Search fields display above the [[CUSIP]] List for a given balance.  
Various search criteria allow you to narrow your results. For example, you can sort the list to see only CUSIPs 
with activity or to limit the list to a particular issue and sub-issue type. 
Note 
The Date field defaults to the current business date – one business date or BD-1. This is known as the Report Date. For 
example, today’s date is 9/20/2022 but we are confirming balances with a Report date of  9/19/2022 close of business.

QUICK ACTIONS AND NAVIGATION 
Quick Actions allow you to go directly to a [[CUSIP]] Detail screen. Type the first 6 digits of the [[CUSIP]] to see a 
look up list from which you may select. If the [[CUSIP]] you select belongs to another account, you will be 
prompted to select that account if you have access to it. 
For those Transfer Agents that have multiple accounts, the Edit icon next to the Agent Account  allows you to 
switch accounts.  
 
Quick Actions Allow You Look Up A Single [[CUSIP]] and the Edit Icon Lets You Switch Agent Accounts 
Standard Navigation 
Several navigational features are available to work with in the [[CUSIP]] list. Export, sort, filter, and page-through 
navigation buttons are available to work with this data. If your results will likely exceed 10,000 records, please 
narrow your search results using the [[CUSIP]] search before exporting.  
 
The [[CUSIP]] List Allows You To  Export Up to 10,000 [[CUSIP]] Results to an Excel file, CSV, and PDF.
