WELCOME
Welcome to the Clearing Fund Management help. Enter a term in the search field to access information, or click an

About the [[DTC]] Clearing Fund Management Application
Use the [[DTC]] Clearing Fund Management application to manage the [[DTC]] Participants Fund deposit accounts in real-
time. View and manage your Participants Fund requirements, deposits, deficits or excesses, and any actions required
on your part. With this application, you can calculate the effect that withdrawal transactions have on your Participants
Fund deposit.
Benefits and Eligibility
The benefits of the [[DTC]] Clearing Fund Management system include:
l Real-time access to the Participants Fund requirements and deposits, and associated reporting.
l Calculation of the effect on your required Participants Fund required deposit based on cash transactions.

Rejecting Requests
[[DTC]] Participants can reject pending cash withdrawals before they are approved/completed. Participants can only
reject requests submitted by users within their own firms; requests submitted by [[DTCC]] Treasury cannot be rejected.

transaction type to select the pending transaction that you want to reject. To reject all selected transaction
types, click the top box. If the status of the pending transaction is Pending Execution, the request cannot be
rejected.

3. To confirm the rejection, clickContinue when asked if you want to proceed with the rejection.
