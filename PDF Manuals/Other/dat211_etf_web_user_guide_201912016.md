[[ETF]]  PORTFOLIO SUBSCRIPTION INQUIRY
The [[ETF]] Portfolio Subscription Inquiry page is used to subscribe and unsubscribe to all available portfolios. 
[[DTCC]] members and Non-Members need to subscribe to a portfolio to view or download the details for that 
subscribed portfolio. You can subscribe to all portfolios or select individual portfolios as long as your client 
account number has the necessary access. During the portfolio distribution process that occurs on T-1 and T, 
[[ETF]] distributes the appropriate portfolios to each subscriber. Subscribers are charged a daily fee for each 
portfolio subscription.
You can search the last five trade dates to view all available subscriptions permissible from your [[ETF]] Portfolio 
[[ID]].
Access the [[ETF]] Portfolio Subscription Inquiry page daily to view the appropriate portfolio subscription 
information in the Enhanced Consolidated PCF Output format.
The following subscription options are available:
 l Auto Subscribe   – Automatically subscribe to all current standard portfolios  and any new future portfolio that are submitted  by all agents.
 l Subscription by Agent  – Automatically subscribe to all current standard portfolios and any new future portfolio that may be submitted for a particular agent.
 l [[BASIC]] Subscription – Subscribe to individual portfolios that you select from the search results.
 l Subscribe to Results – Subscribes you to  all [[ETF]] portfolios in the search results, not just what is visible on the current page.

Subscribing to Standard Portfolios
You have permission to view and download only the information for the portfolios that you have set up a 
subscription. You can subscribe to all portfolios  or  select individual portfolios. 
Note
You must wait 24 hours between subscribing and unsubscribing to a portfolio.
Setting up Auto Subscribe
You can automatically subscribe to all of the portfolios or baskets by all of the agents, which is called Auto 
Subscribe. You can also auto subscribe to specific [[ETF]] agents, which is called Agent Subscription. The 
benefit of auto subscribing to all of the portfolios is that new portfolios are automatically added to your 
subscription. If you have not auto subscribed, you must manually subscribe to each new portfolio. To 
unsubscribe to individual portfolios, you must turn off the auto subscribe feature. 
To auto subscribe to all portfolios:
 1. Login to the Exchange Traded Funds ([[ETF]]) home page. See Accessing the Exchange Traded Fund ([[ETF]]) Web. The following figure shows the Exchange Traded Funds ([[ETF]]) home page:
 2. From the [[ETF]] Portfolio Subscription Inquiry page, the [[default]], click Auto Subscribe.
