LENS HELP 
Welcome to LENS 
Every day issuers, agents and courts produce an abundance of [[securities]]-related notices — everything from 
bankruptcies and defaults, to class actions and Section 19(a) notices. 
[[DTCC]] houses one of the most comprehensive repositories of legal notices in the U.S.; and LENS lets you 
access the notices you need soon after they’re available to us. This streamlined, centralized online portal 
makes every notice in the [[DTCC]] library available to you in PDF form. 
 
Notice Types  
The LENS library contains documents categorized as one of the three notice types: Legal, [[transfer agent]], or 
Tax. When searching for notices in LENS, you may limit your search to any of the categories shown below. 
Legal (LG) 
 Notices to Security Holders 
 Bankruptcies 
 Litigation / Class Actions

Welcome to Viewing Lens Notices. This video is the first video in a two-part series on LENS. Refer to all the 
LENS topics for more information. 
The Legal Notice System allows users to view, download, and search for notices concerning [[DTC]]- eligible 
[[securities]]. The LENS homepage or dashboard is composed of three areas: 
1. First, the notice grid allows immediate access to notices posted today, yesterday, and notices posted 
since the last time you logged into the application. These categories are available for timely retrieval of 
notices to facilitate the workflows for active users. Additionally, the grid can be customized to display 
notices based off of your Preferences settings.  
2. Second, the bulk download feature allows you to download up to 1,000 notices at once. The bulk 
download feature can also be customized to download only CUSIPs or notice types of interest. 
3. Finally, the notice search allows you to search by [[CUSIP]], keyword, notice type or LENS document 
number. The search can retrieve notices up to five years in the past. 
 
For the purposes of this webcast, we will focus on the notice grid. To learn about the bulk download and notice 
search, see the Download and Notice Search topics. 
Three types of LENS notices are offered and appear along the left side of the dashboard grid: 
 Legal notices, 
 [[transfer agent]] notices, and 
 Tax Information notices. 
 
To the right of the notice types, the number of notices posted Today, Yesterday, or since your Last Login are 
presented. Simply click a number and the notices for that category are displayed.

LENS Help 7 
To open a notice, click any document number in your results. A pdf opens containing the notice information. 
The results list also shows additional information such as the [[CUSIP]] or [[CUSIP]] family, notice sub-type, and a 
description of the notice. 
When a notice references multiple CUSIPs, a plus sign appears next to the [[CUSIP]]. Click the plus sign to see 
all the CUSIPs that apply to the particular notice. 
To return to the Dashboard, click Home. 
Now let’s look more closely at the grid columns on the dashboard. 
The Today column updates to show notices as they are posted to LENS throughout the day. The cutoff for 
notices to stop appearing under the Today column is mid night EST.  After midnight, Today’s notices roll under 
Yesterday’s notices and appear there. 
 Yesterday’s notices show the total number of notices that were announced the previous day. That number 
will stay the same throughout the day, if you prefer to work off a fixed list, then clicking on the Yesterday 
link would be best. Using the grid this way, can help you to track notices on an ongoing basis. 
 Finally, the Since Last Login column saves up all the notices that were posted since you last logged into 
LENS. This insures you will not miss a notice while on vacation or following a holiday. 
 As a global organization, [[DTC]] will post notices to LENS Monday through Friday, and even on some U.S. 
holidays, with no interruption of service. 
 
Keep in mind that you can download all the notices you see in the grid by using the bulk download feature. Per 
notice pricing rules apply if your firm does not have a LENS subscription, to verify if your firm has an existing 
subscription please consult your Relationship Manager or email us at lensweb@[[DTCC]].com. See the download 
topic for more information.  
Finally, there is a way to filter the grid so only those notices or CUSIPS from your Preferences are shown.  
Click the Preferences button. See how the counts reduce? In certain categories there is a zero. This is 
because you have turned on your Preferences.  
Working with the Preferences filter will streamline your view so that you can focus on only the notices that are 
relevant to you.  
You are able to see your preferred notices that were posted Today, Yesterday, and Since your Last Login.  
Note 
The Preferences filter does not apply to the Search Notices section of the Dashboard. 
To return to the regular LENS view, click All Notices.   
The Preference filter will only work if your preferences are set up. To set up your preferences, click the 
Preferences Link on the menu bar.  
For more information on setting up preferences, see the Preferences topic.

Preferences 
Setting up your LENS preferences allows you to [[DO]] the following:  
 Establish a list of CUSIPs and / or notice types of interest that are saved as your preferences. 
 Receive email notifications on a daily or weekly basis, when notices that match your preferences are 
posted in LENS. You may also receive this information in a CSV spreadsheet. 
 Filter your preferences on the LENS dashboard. 
 Bulk download notices based on preferences. 
Setting Up Your Preferences 
Setting up preferences is a two-part process. To set up general preference settings, from the menu bar select 
Preferences>Alerts and Settings. To create a custom list of CUSIPs that can be optionally used to refine your 
preferences further, select Preferences>[[CUSIP]] Interest List.

1. From the Preferences menu, select Alerts and Settings.

here, you may indicate whether you wish to receive all CUSIPs or limit to only CUSIPs from your

button. From here click the checkboxes to customize. 
 
 
4. Click Submit. Then click OK to confirm. 
A message displays that your preferences were successfully set up and or changed.

Creating a [[CUSIP]] Interest List 
Use this procedure to indicate specific CUSIPs for your preferences. Add up to 500 CUSIPs at a time here; 
additional CUSIPs can be added with another submission. 
When in effect, your [[CUSIP]] List preferences apply to:

2. Click the Add CUSIPs button on the upper right of the page.

paste CUSIPs directly from Excel. Instead, use a program such as Notepad to remove hidden characters.

Downloading Notices in Bulk 
1. From the LENS Dashboard, click the “+” sign to the right of Notice Download (bulk).  
The bulk download area is now viewable. 
2. Select a download notice type.  
Choices are LG-Legal, TA-[[transfer agent]], or TX-Tax Information. 
3. Select download criteria.  
Choices are Today, Yesterday or Since Last Login*.   
* displays only if notices are available since last login. 
4. Click the Download button.  
Note 
The button will not be active if you are attempting to download a type of notice that is not currently available. 
A message displays confirming you wish to proceed.  
5. To cancel the bulk Download, click Cancel.   
6. To proceed with the bulk Download, click OK.

Downloading Notices Individually 
You may select individual notices to download from your search results page.  
1. From the LENS dashboard, either select a hyperlink in the Dashboard grid, or search for notices via the 
Notice Search section. Either one of these actions will advance you to the results page. 
2. From your results, click the check boxes on the left to select notices for download.  
3. To select all the notices in the results, click the box at the top of the column. 
4. Click the Download Selected button. 
 
Multiple notices are downloaded in a zip file. A single notice downloads as an Acrobat .pdf file. You are 
prompted to open or save the file(s) to your computer.
