[[DTCC]] Internal (Green) 
MFPS II, Profile Security Database – Input / Output Transmission Schedule 
 
Fund batch file input will be accepted by [[NSCC]] between 6:00 AM ET and 7:00 PM ET in the Production region and 6:00AM and 2:00PM 
ET in the PSE region. This will be available on all [[NSCC]] business days. 
 
One extraction of the output will be available by 9:00 PM ET from the Production region and 4:00PM ET from the PSE region... This will 
be available on all [[NSCC]] business days. Rejected records in PROD will be distributed back to the originator by [[NSCC]] by 9:00PM ET and 
in PSE by 4:00PM ET.
