About Full Disclosure 5 
ABOUT FULL DISCLOSURE 
How the Networking System Relates to Networking Level 3 Accounts 
Networking is a centralized and standardized data communications system for the exchange of customer 
account level activity. Networking was implemented in 1988 to permit the transmission of data between 
participants to reconcile the [[Broker]]/Dealer’s (“Firm”) records with the Fund’s records. 
Networking is the industry standard for the exchange of mutual fund customer account maintenance and 
reporting. The Networking system allows non-trade related client information to appear identically on the records 
of 
• Firms and Funds, or 
• Transfer agents for Firms and Funds 
 
With Networking, Firms and Funds can exchange timely and accurate information about their customers, in a 
controlled environment. 
For Networking Levels 3 or 4, a Firm establishes its accounts on the books of the Fund. The Firm is in essence the 
shareholder on the account. 
In Networking Level 3, the Firm maintains and controls its Accounts and informs the Fund of the accounts’ activity. 
Full disclosure and the addition of related SSN records to Fund/SERV® and Networking (order and new account) will 
allow for increased linking opportunities using SSN, customer name, and/or common address at the Fund’s transfer 
agent. 
Full disclosure refers to a client’s: 
• Name 
• Legal account type (e.g., joint tenant, trust, IRA, etc.) 
• Address 
• Social Security Number (“SSN”) 
• Account type code (“[[NSCC]] Social Code”)

Best Practices 
This section will lay out the various scenarios for doing Networking Level 3 with full disclosure. The best 
practices guidelines will satisfy the [[transfer agent]] searches recommended by the Task Force. The client data 
share will include the following: 
1. Existing client file from Firm to Fund 
2. Reporting of new Networking Level 3 accounts from Firm to Fund 
Existing Client Account from Firm to Fund 
• Firms will submit a Networking Conversion Update (B51) record containing the full name, address, 
SSN/TIN, social code, etc. for all Networking Level 3 accounts maintained on their books.  
• Coordination between Funds, Firms and [[DTCC]] is recommended. Firms should provide volume estimates.  
• Firms should consider sending a “test” file before releasing all of their data.

System Functions - Funds 10 
SYSTEM FUNCTIONS - FUNDS 
Conversion & New Account Processing 
Conversion Processing 
Funds will use the data from the Firm Conversion records (Record Type B51) to establish account linkages. 
Each fund company is in the best position to know and apply their prospectus linking rules. 
Initially, all client registration information that is on the Firm’s books for Networking Level 3 accounts will come 
to the Fund in a one-time load using B51 records. Funds and firms should coordinate the timing of their 
transmissions and inform [[DTCC]]. 
• Record Type B51 is used when a Firm is updating a Networking Level 3 Account.  
New Account Processing 
Funds will use the data from the Networking New Account (B50) or Fund/SERV® Registration (401) records to 
establish account linkages. Each fund company is in the best position to know and apply their prospectus 
linking rules. 
• Record Type B50 is used to establish a new account via Networking. 
• Fund/SERV® order record (001) and full registration record (401) is used to esta blish a new account via 
Fund/SERV®. 
• ACATS transfer record (018) is used to establish a new account via ACATS.

Reference Guide 11 
REFERENCE GUIDE 
Table 3 serves as a reference for specific issues that may arise as a result of Firms providing full disclosure 
information to Fund Families. 
 
Table 3 Reference Guide 
Term Description 
Brokerage Custodian 
IRA 
Accounts 
Firms should include their custodian/trustee information as part of the registration disclosure 
(e.g., ABC [[securities]] Cust for). In addition, the [[Beneficial Owner]]’s SSN and address should be 
provided as opposed to the custodian’s. 
Social Code 
Disclosure 
Firms should provide the most applicable [[NSCC]] Social Code as part of the Order and New 
Account records.  Firms may need to program a mapping routine to cross-reference their 
brokerage account type indicators to the most valid [[NSCC]] Social Code. Fund company transfer 
agents should perform reporting based upon Networking Level, regardless of social code. 
Address and Zip Code 
Disclosure 
Firms should provide a client address and Zip Code standardized in the proper United States 
format. The Zip Plus 4 (“extended zip”) should be supplied as formatted. The inclusion of the 
“delivery point extension” (Zip plus 4 plus 2) is encouraged when applicable (apartment 
buildings, etc.). 
Shareowner 
Confirmation 
Statements 
A Firm’s full disclosure of client registration and address does not change the existing media 
suppression of financial and non-financial confirm mailings. Fund company transfer agents 
should suppress all statements on Networking Level 3 positions. 
Shareowner Periodic 
Statements 
A Firm’s full disclosure of client registration and address does not change the existing media 
suppression of periodic (monthly, quarterly, annual) mailings. Fund company transfer agents 
should suppress all statements on Networking Level 3 positions. 
Tax Reporting A Firm’s full disclosure of client registration, address, and SSN does not change the existing 
responsibilities of tax reporting mailings or filings. Fund company transfer agents should 
exclude all Networking Level 3 positions from tax reporting. 
Tax Withholding A Firm’s full disclosure of client registration, address, and SSN does not change the historic 
process of withholding on Networking Level 3 positions. Fund company transfer agents should 
apply withholding on distributions and/or redemptions based only on the receipt of an applicable 
Withhold indicator. Furthermore, all withholding proceeds should be forwarded to the dealer 
home office for appropriate reporting to the IRS and other authorities on behalf of the client. 
Customer 
Identification Program 
(CIP) 
A Firm’s full disclosure of client registration, address, and SSN does not change the 
responsibility  of CIP. Fund company transfer agents should exclude all Networking Level 3 
positions from CIP.
