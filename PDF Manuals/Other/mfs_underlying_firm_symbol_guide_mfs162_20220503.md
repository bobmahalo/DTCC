Overview 5 
OVERVIEW 
In June of 2004 the ICI's Bank, Trust, & Retirement Advisory Committee (BTRAC) Best Practices taskforce 
recommended [[NSCC]] create a method of identifying underlying Banks, Trusts, TPAs, Plan Sponsors, Insurance 
[[Companies]] and Consultants who are not [[NSCC]] members but clear through an [[NSCC]] [[Member]]. The objective of 
this taskforce was to develop a standard symbol that would identify underlying entities, excluding [[Broker]] dealers, 
within fund accounts. Ideally, both funds and firms would use this symbol within Fund/SERV®, Networking and 
SDR layouts to provide greater transparency of [[intermediary]] relationships on fund accounts. 
Members of the BTRAC committee were asked to provide lists of Banks, Trusts, TPAs and other entities to the 
taskforce. The various lists were consolidated into one comprehensive list and an identifier was assigned to each 
entity. The Underlying Firm Symbol (UFS) taskforce reviewed the list and worked with [[DTCC]] to provide a central 
location to maintain the list. 
The list of intermediaries and UFS identifiers are available at the Mutual Fund Client Documentation section of the 
[[DTCC]] Learning Center (username and password required). Maintenance of the UFS list is offered through [[DTCC]] 
Solutions LLC, a wholly-owned subsidiary of [[DTCC]]. 
This User Guide provides guidelines for using the UFS and describes the process for requesting changes to the 
UFS List. The User Guide will be edited as needed to reflect enhancements to UFS usage or changes to the UFS 
List maintenance process.

Usage 6 
USAGE 
UFS Usage 
Underlying Firm Symbols are available in Fund/SERV®, Networking and [[DTCC]] Payment aXis to identify 
financial intermediaries that are not themselves [[NSCC]] members but process orders through [[NSCC]] [[Member]]. 
Examples of usage are to identify underlying firms at the time of account setup through [[NSCC]] 401 and 
A/B/T50,51 records, communicate this information through account reconciliation records such as Retirement 
Plan Reporting, [[DTCC]] Payment aXis as well as provide needed transparency on some omnibus and super 
omnibus accounts as defined within the Networking Standardized Data Reporting (SDR) process (see below). 
Standardized Data Reporting (SDR) Usage 
An example of how UFS is utilized for SDR would be in response (typically a Summary response) to an initial 
data request from a fund, where the first [[intermediary]] will identify second or third tier intermediaries on accounts 
by entering either the [[NSCC]] Number, [[OTC]] Symbol (for underlying [[Broker]] dealers), or UFS (other underlying 
entities) in the Underlying Firm field on SDR record type B25 layout. Subsequently, the fund may use the 
underlying firm data received to narrow down its follow-up request for Detail data. For more information on SDR 
procedures, please refer to the SDR Best Practices and SDR Technical Guide within the Learning center 
https://dtcclearning.com/products-and-services/mutual-fund-services/mfs-cross-products/underlying-firm-symbols-
ufs.html

UFS List Ownership/Maintenance Requirements 
The UFS List was created through collaboration with [[DTCC]] clients/users and the Investment Company 
Institute Bank, Trust & Retirement Advisory Committee (BTRAC). [[DTCC]] Solutions LLC, a subsidiary of 
[[DTCC]], maintains the list on behalf of its clients. References in this document to “[[DTCC]]” include its 
subsidiaries. [[DTCC]] maintains the UFS list as a central r esource for its [[NSCC]] members. [[NSCC]] members 
are responsible for the accuracy of the information they provide for the UFS List. This section of the User 
Guide will provide a process and the guidelines for making changes to the UFS List. Please note the 
following rules as they pertain to making changes to the UFS List:  
Only [[NSCC]] members may request changes to the list. 
• No [[Broker]] dealers, Banks, Trusts, or TPAs that are members of [[NSCC]] should be 
added/included on the UFS list, as a standardized mechanism alread y exists to identify them: the 
[[NSCC]] number.  
• No entities that have an [[OTC]] Symbol or existing UFS should be added.   Any organization submitting 
a request for a new UFS must review [[DTCC]]’s lists for all 3 identifiers to ensure  that the entity does 
not already have its own unique number.  These lists can be found at the following links: 
 
➢ The [[NSCC]] [[Member]] and [[NSCC]] [[OTC]] Directories can be found on [[DTCC]].com website  (Note: 
[[OTC]] Symbols are under MPID Directory listing). 
➢ UFS List is located on the [[DTCC]] Learning site at https://dtcclearning.com/products -and-
services/mutual -fund-services/mfs -cross-products/underlying -firm-symbols-ufs.html 
 
• No [[nominee]] or [[nominee]] Names should be used when adding a bank.  
• Provide the legal entity of the firm.  Acronyms or abbreviated versions should not be used.  
• There will be three types of list maintenance  requests available to [[NSCC]] members: ADD, MODIFY, 
and DEACTIVATE.  
• The City and State of the entity will be used in determining duplicate listings. For example, if a 
[[Member]] requests to add ABC TPA in Denver, CO, and ABC TPA in Phoenix, AZ. exists on the list, a 
new symbol will be  assigned to ABC TPA in Denver, CO.   
 
NOTE:  In keeping with ‘Know Your Customer’ efforts, submitted UFS requests are to include full street address of entity to 
be added (not P.O. Box, R.R #, etc.) 
• In certain circumstances, a submitter may request a second number for an entity that has a current 
UFS if the second number is for a distinct business line or a separate geographic location. In these 
instances, the request must clearly explain the reason f or requesting a second UFS.  
• [[DTCC]] will make changes to the list after receiving a maintenance  request form in good order. 
• [[DTCC]] will publish a new updated list at the beginning of each month (if additions or modifications 
were made in previous month).  
• All changes will be reviewed periodically throughout the year by the BTRAC Committee and may be 
rejected subsequently.

UFS List Maintenance Form 
Submission of a maintenance request form is required to make changes to the existing UFS List  The 
three types of main tenances are ADD, MODIFY, and DEACTIVATE.  These all have separate tabs on the 
same form; please make sure you are using the correct tab.   The required form is a Microsoft Excel file 
that supports requests for either a single underlying firm or a batch re quest for adding or deactivating 
multiple underlying firms. This UFS Request and Maintenance Form used for submitting all 
maintenance requests can be found on Learning under Underling Firm Symbols  in the [[Mutual Funds]] 
section: https://dtcclearning.com/products -and-services/mutual -fund-services/mfs -cross-
products/underlying -firm-symbols-ufs.html 
Note 
These documents are available  to [[NSCC]] members only. Please contact Wealth Management Services at 212-855-8877 to 
obtain a user name and password or if you need assistance regarding the UFS maintenance form or listing. 
All fields in the Requestor Information section are required to be completed for a change to the list to be 
completed. The Contact Information is especially critical in the event the BTRAC Comm ittee subsequently 
rejects a change during its periodic review.  
In addition to the rules cited above in UFS List Ownership/Maintenance Requirements, the file includes 
specific instructions that must be followed to support timely and accurate processing.

ADDITIONAL INFORMATION 
Banks, Trusts, Retirement Advisory Committee (BTRAC) 
The ICI BTRAC meets several times per year where a review of all maintenance requests received since 
the previous review/meeting is done.  
Reminder 
Changes are made promptly to the list by [[DTCC]] if the maintenance request form is received in good order. 
For any questionable item, a [[Member]] of the Committee will contact the person identified in the Contact 
Information fields within the Requestor Information section of the form. Based on  the outcome of the 
discussion, the committee will decide whether to accept or reject a request.  
The BTRAC Committee has the final right to refuse any maintenance request to the list.  
UFS Format 
The UFS is 4 bytes in l ength with the following format: Alpha  only, Numeric only, Alphanumeric, 
Alphanumeric.  
This format was designed to minimize the risk overlapping with other identifiers such as [[NSCC]] numbers, 
which currently are all numeric, and [[OTC]] symbols, which are currently all alpha.  
[[DTCC]] is the only party that may assign UFS identifiers based on requests presented in accordance with 
the instructions in this User Guide and those included in the electronic file.
