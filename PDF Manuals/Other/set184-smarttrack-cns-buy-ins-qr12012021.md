WELCOME TO SMART/TRACK FOR BUY-INS 
The SMART/Track for Buy-ins Service automates the processing of [[CNS]] and Non-[[CNS]] buy-in and MSRB 
close-out notices. Originators of buy-in notices can create, transmit, cancel, or update notices. Receivers of 
notices can accept or reject notices. The service tracks a notice throughout its life cycle, storing all information 
on the notice online for seven years. 
All features are available through the SMART/Track browser or through file and message transmissions. Both 
[[Broker]]-to-[[Broker]] and [[Broker]]-to-[[CNS]] transactions as well as MSRB close-out notices are eligible for processing.  
ABOUT THIS GUIDE 
The purpose of this guide is to explain the process for submitting buy-ins to [[NSCC]]’s [[CNS]] (Continuous Net 
[[settlement]] system) using the SMART/Track for Buy-ins application. Included are the steps and timelines for 
[[CNS]] buy-ins and retransmittals, cutoff times for submission, and answers to frequently asked questions. 
For a detailed explanation of the SMART/Track for Buy-ins system, refer to the SMART/Track for Buy-ins User 
Guide or click the Help button in the browser application. For more information about the Continuous Net 
[[settlement]] System, refer to the [[settlement]] Service Guide at: 
http://www.[[DTCC]].com/~/media/Files/Downloads/legal/service-guides/[[settlement]].pdf?la=en

Frequently Asked Questions 6 
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 
How much can I submit the [[CNS]] buy-in intent for? 
A [[CNS]] buy-in notice may be submitted for the closing [[CNS]] position minus any prior intents in the system.  
After I’ve submitted the [[CNS]] notice of intent to buy-in, how long does the short [[Broker]] have to satisfy 
its [[short position]]? 
From the date of Notification to the end of Notice + 2. For more information, refer to the [[CNS]] Buy -in Timeline 
on page 4. 
I received a [[CNS]] Buy-in Liability notification. What actions can I take to avoid the buy-in being 
executed against me? 
Deliver the [[shares]] to [[CNS]]. 
Does a long settling trade or a long miscellaneous activity reduce my liability on an outstanding buy -
in? 
No. If you [[DO]] have [[shares]] to deliver to satisfy your buy-in liability, you must contact [[DTCC]] Cashiering 
Operations to expedite the delivery in a timely manner. 
I delivered the [[shares]] to [[CNS]]. Am I still subject to the buy-in? 
Once you have delivered the [[shares]] equaling your liability before the Valued Recycle Cutoff, you are no longer 
subject to the buy-in. 
I executed a buy-in but forgot to submit the [[CNS]] buy-in order. Am I covered? 
Unfortunately you are not covered. You must first submit a [[CNS]] buy-in order by 10 a.m. on the day of 
execution to execute a [[CNS]] buy-in. 
How [[DO]] I submit an executed buy-in to [[CNS]]? 
After you execute the buy-in in the marketplace, you must submit the execution notice to [[CNS]] through 
SMART/Track. Participants are responsible to verify that the execution notice has been accepted by [[CNS]].  
How [[DO]] I know if a [[CNS]] buy-in was executed against me? 
Participants should check SMART/Track periodically to see if a buy-in was executed against them. Also, the 
execution will appear on the [[CNS]] Miscellaneous Activity report as a code 77.  
A [[CNS]] buy-in was executed against me at a price that I don’t agree with. What should I [[DO]]? 
Contact the executing [[Broker]] and work with the [[Broker]]’s firm to resolve any disagreement. If you [[DO]] not know 
who the executing [[Broker]] is, contact [[DTCC]] Cashiering Operations.

Frequently Asked Questions 7 
When [[DO]] I submit a [[CNS]] retransmittal notice to [[CNS]]? 
If you receive a buy-in notice outside of [[CNS]], you may retransmit the buy-in to [[CNS]] before 12:00 Noon on the 
day before the buy-in is due for execution outside of [[CNS]]. 
If I owe [[CNS]] [[shares]], how [[DO]] I know if a [[CNS]] retransmittal notice was issued against me?  
Participants should check SMART/Track periodically to see if a retransmittal was iss ued against them. 
What are the cutoff times for [[CNS]] intents, retransmittals, orders and executions?  
You can submit a buy-in intent to [[CNS]] through SMART/Track by 6:00 p.m. Eastern time or by 5:45 p.m. 
Eastern time for file transmissions. [[CNS]] buy-in intents remain in SMART/Track with a Pending status until they 
are sent to [[CNS]] after 6:00 p.m. Eastern time. 
You can submit a [[CNS]] retransmittal by 12 noon Eastern time on the day before execution. Retransmittals can 
only be transmitted through the browser application of SMART/Track and not by file transmission. 
Retransmittals are sent to [[CNS]] as soon as they are received in SMART/Track.  
You can submit a [[CNS]] buy-in order by 10:00 a.m. Eastern time or by 9:45 a.m. Eastern time for file 
transmissions on Notice + 2. [[CNS]] buy-in orders remain in SMART/Track with a Pending status until they are 
sent to [[CNS]] after 10:00 a.m. Eastern time. 
You can submit a [[CNS]] buy-in execution by 6:00 p.m. Eastern time on Notice + 2. Executions can only be 
transmitted through the browser application of SMART/Track and not by file transmission. Executions are sent 
to [[CNS]] as soon as they are received in SMART/Track. 
When are [[CNS]] buy-in notices archived? 
[[CNS]] buy-in intents, orders, retransmittals and executions are automatically archived 60 days after the creation 
date. 
SMART/Track maintains an online archive of all notices for 7 years. 
I have someone who needs access to SMART/Track for Buy-ins. How [[DO]] I go about getting them 
access? 
To register for SMART/Track for Buy-ins, each [[participant]] firm must complete an access coordinator and online 
registration form. After these steps are complete, a user [[ID]] and temporary password are emailed to you.  
Step One: Submit an access coordinator form. 
1. Request an Access Coordinator form online or from your [[DTCC]] Account Executive within [[DTCC]]’s 
Relationship Services. To request the form online, go to http://www.[[DTCC]].com. From the Customer Center 
menu, choose Access Coordinator Forms. 
2. Fill in the names, telephone numbers, e-mail addresses, and street addresses of the people at your firm to 
be designated as access coordinators (at least two). 
3. Obtain the signature of an authorizing officer. The authorized signature cannot be provided by any of the 
designated access coordinators. 
4. Affix the medallion guarantee stamp.
