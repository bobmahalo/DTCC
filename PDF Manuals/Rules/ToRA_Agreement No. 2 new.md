1.  Definitions 
 
 
As used in this Exhibit A, the following terms have the meanings set forth below.  Unless otherwise 
indicated, all other capitalized terms contained herein shall have the same meanings as set forth in the 
Transfer of Retirement Assets Agreement executed by the undersigned as of                      , 
   (“Agreement”). 
 
 
1.      “Account Detail” means the following information relating to an Account: the Account 
number; the Accountholder's name and federal taxpayer identification number; the type of Plan 
maintained by the Accountholder; and the [[CUSIP]] number of the Fund [[shares]] involved in a Transfer. 
 
 
2.   “Acknowledgement Record” means an Instruction from the Delivering Institution to the 
Receiving Institution that contains the Accountholder's current Account Detail and the amount of the 
proposed Transfer (in dollar and/or share amounts) as maintained on the records of the Delivering

3. “Confirmation Date” means the first Business Day immediately following the 
Acknowledgment Date (as defined in Section 2.2(b) of this Exhibit A).

5.      “[[CUSIP]] number” means the identification number of Fund [[shares]] provided by Standard & 
Poor's Committee on Uniform [[securities]] Identification Procedures. 
 
 
6. “Final NAV Calculation” means to the final time on the Confirmation Date as of which a 
Fund calculates its net [[Asset]] value per share for purposes of Rule 22c-1. 
 
 
7.     “Good Order Transfer Initiation Record” means a Transfer Initiation Record that (a) is received 
from a Receiving Institution that the Delivering Institution determines in its sole discretion to be an 
Institution with which the Delivering Institution wishes to process Transfers through the Service, (b) meets 
the requirements as established by the [[NSCC]], and (c) contains Account Detail that is consistent with the 
Account information maintained in the records of the Delivering Institution. 
 
 
8. “[[settlement]] Date” means the Business Day immediately following the Confirmation Date.

Final NAV Calculation of a Fund, the Delivering Institution shall on the Confirmation Date 
(1) effect the Transfer with respect to that Fund by redeeming [[shares]] on the Confirmation 
Date from the Accountholder's Account in the amount indicated in the Transfer Initiation 
Record at the net [[Asset]] value per share of that Fund determined in connection with its Final 
NAV Calculation, (2) withhold the federal income taxes as specified in the Transfer Initiation 
Record, and (3) transmit a Confirmation Record to the Receiving Institution.
