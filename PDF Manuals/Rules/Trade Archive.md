Trade Archive
With the shift to T+1, the [[SEC]] adopted amendments to [[Rule 204]]-2 of the Advisers Act
to require [[SEC]]-registered investment advisers (RIAs), for any transaction that is
subject to Rule 15c6-2(a), to retain records of confirmations received, and any
allocation and each affirmation sent or received, each with a date and time stamp.
As such, [[DTCC]] ITP is expanding archival services for Institutional clients who are
subscribers of CTM and TradeSuite [[ID]].  Trade Archive will store the trade records from
CTM and TradeSuite [[ID]] in a centralized cloud-based system on a go forward basis with
the ability to filter, sort and download data that include the [[Rule 204]]-2 required trade
information.
