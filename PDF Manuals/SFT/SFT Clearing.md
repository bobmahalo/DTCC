The [[SFT]] Clearing service introduces central clearing for [[Equity]] [[securities]] financing
transactions, including lending, borrowing and Repo to:
Support central clearing of institutional clients’ [[Equity]] SFTs intermediated by sponsoring
members.
Support central clearing of [[Equity]] SFTs between full service [[NSCC]] members.
Maximize capital efficiency and mitigates systemic risks by introducing more
membership and cleared transaction opportunities for market participants.
The key parameters of the [[SFT]] Clearing service include:
Requirement to submit SFTs to [[NSCC]] on a locked-in/matched basis by an approved
submitter.
All [[SFT]] activity has a term with overnight being supported on day 1. Settlements are
allowed to [[pair off]] daily against new activity  to minimize the operational burden of
settling overnight [[obligations]]. [[NSCC]] calculates and processes price
differences/[[mark-to-market]] that are created by the daily [[pair off]].
