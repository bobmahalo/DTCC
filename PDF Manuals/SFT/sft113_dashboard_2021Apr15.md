INTRODUCTION
The [[securities]] Financing Transaction ([[SFT]]) Clearing service provides central clearing for [[Equity]] lending and 
borrowing transactions. The service utilizes the clearing capabilities, risk management, and infrastructure of 
the National [[securities]] Clearing Corporation ([[NSCC]]). While [[NSCC]] full-service members and institutional 
clients should receive real-time message updates about status changes, the [[SFT]] Clearing service 
consolidates your data and enables you to:
 l View your [[SFT]] data through a dashboard.
 l Filter and sort your transactions.
 l Export data to a Microsoft Excel file for further analysis.

Clearing Corporation ([[NSCC]]). The Loan Status page enables you to track these transactions and provides 
you with a  high-level view of loans of [[Equity]] [[securities]] and enables you to drill-down to individual loan details. 
You can filter the search results for your associated accounts to display the data or export all loan status 
details based on the filters to an Excel file.

yet settled.
 l SETTLED – An on-leg or off-leg loan transaction in the SAS system that resulted 
in a [[DO]] or Price Differential submitted to [[DTC]] that settled.
 l TERMINATED – An off-leg loan transaction in the SAS system where a 
termination instruction was received from the Approved Trade Submitter that 
resulted in the loan being removed from the [[SFT]] Clearing service.

Recalls
Recall notices are sent out from the lender to the [[Borrower]] and facilitated by the [[NSCC]] as a counterparty. As 
per the [[securities]] Lending Agreement (SLA) guidelines, these notices state what [[shares]] are being recalled 
and when they are due to return from the [[Borrower]]. [[SFT]] Clearing members can search and track the status of 
any recall your firm sent to borrowers or received from lenders using the [[SFT]] Clearing Dashboard. 
You can view the long buy-in recall position and position details. You can filter the search results for display on 
the page and export the data to an Excel file.
To view the recalls:
 1. From the home page, select Search > Recalls, as shown in the following figure:
 2. From the Recalls page,  if you have one [[SFT]] Clearing [[Member]] [[ID]], the field is automatically prepopulated
