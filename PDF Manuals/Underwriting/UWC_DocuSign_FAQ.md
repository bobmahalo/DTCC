Summary 4 
SUMMARY 
The [[Depository Trust Company]] (“[[DTC]]”) is developing a new application, [[Underwriting]] Central (UWC), which 
will eventually replace UW SOURCE. The first phase of the rewrite, which will be released in Q1 of 2021, is 
specific to Retail Certificates of Deposit (CDs). 
[[DTC]] is introducing electronic certificates to replace physical master certificates.  
The DocuSign FAQ guide was developed to help clients better understand the electronic certificate and 
electronic BLOR DocuSign process. 
For additional queries, please contact UWCPM@[[DTCC]].com. 
Note: Phase 1 of the new application is specific to fixed rate, non-[[FAST]] CDs, issued by retail banks.  
Note:  
Phase 1 of the new application is specific to fixed rate, non-[[FAST]] CDs, issued by retail banks.  
The following CDs are out of scope for Phase 1:  
• [[FAST]],  
• structured CDs,  
• zero-coupon,  
• variable interest rates,  
• certificates issued by Credit Unions, and  
• [[Secondary Offerings]] where the primary [[CUSIP]] was not submitted via the new UWC application. 
Electronic Certificates of Deposit 
Focusing on retail (brokered) CDs, [[DTC]] is introducing electronic certificates to replace physical master 
certificates.  
This new process enables brokers to enter security information into the UWC application via the user interface 
or upload functionality. Once the security information is finalized, the [[Broker]] can systemically generate 
electronic master CDs and direct them to up to 5 contacts at the [[issuer]] using DocuSign.  
The [[issuer]] can then electronically sign, reassign, or decline the electronic certificate. 
Once the [[issuer]] electronically signs the master certificate, [[DTCC]] stores the electronic certificate in an 
electronic vault. A watermarked copy of the Master CD is made available via email to both the [[Broker]] and 
[[issuer]].

[[DTC]], in partnership with the industry, developed (4) standardized master electronic certificate templates for 
this initiative. The templates were driven by the industry need to mimic the existing physical templates. The (4) 
standardized master electronic certificate templates are as follows: 
• [[DTC]] – Form Fixed Rate Noncallable 
• [[DTC]] – Form Fixed Rate Callable 
• [[DTC]] – Form Step Rate Callable 
• [[DTC]] – Form Step Rate Noncallable 
 
The (4) standardized master electronic certificate templates will eventually live on [[DTCC]].com.  
Electronic Blanket Letter of Representation 
Issuers must execute a new Electronic Blanket Letter of Representations (E-CD BLOR) in order to utilize the 
electronic certificate eligibility process. 
UW Product Management will work with issuers to execute an E-CD BLOR prior to the launch of the new 
application.  
The new E-CD BLOR template and Rider A will eventually live on [[DTCC]].com. Underwriters or issuers will be 
able to access the E-CD BLOR from the [[DTCC]] website, and then direct the E-CD BLOR to a specified [[issuer]] 
contact using DocuSign. 
The [[issuer]] will receive an email with the E-CD BLOR. The [[issuer]] contact can then review the E-CD BLOR, 
electronically sign as necessary, and then submit back to [[DTC]] via DocuSign. 
Once the E-CD BLOR is approved by [[DTC]], the E-CD BLOR requirement is satisfied. 
Note:  
Underwriters will have the capability to transmit a BLOR to the [[issuer]] directly from the application.

Q2 Am I the only person at my company to receive the e-mail from 
DocuSign? 
A2 If the BLOR is generated from the external website, then the email can be directed to only one user. 
However, the UW has the [[Option]] to select up to 5 people at your company to receive the e-mail if the 
BLOR is generated from within the application. The first one of you to open the e-mail locks it for use. 
Subsequent attempts to open the certificate for signing will be presented with an alert. 
 
Q3 Nobody on the recipient list has the authorization to sign this form 
and/or I would like to assign the form to another representative at my 
company. What should I [[DO]]? 
A3 Please [[DO]] not forward the DocuSign e-mail you received; that will not properly re-assign the envelope. 
Instead, please follow the process below: 
1. Open the DocuSign envelope and select “Review Document” (from the link you received in the e-
mail). 
2. Once you are in DocuSign, click “Other Actions” at the top right corner of the screen, then click 
“Assign to Someone Else.” 
 
3. Enter the e-mail address and name of the new signer. Optionally, provide a reason for the 
reassignment (i.e., “Assigning to legal department”).

Q19 Will I have to submit another BLOR? 
A19 As part of the new application, a new BLOR will be required for each 6-digit [[CUSIP]] base. If there is a 
BLOR on file for the 6-digit base, the legal requirement is satisfied.  
A new BLOR will be required when the [[issuer]] commences issuing with a new 6-digit base. [[DTC]] and 
the [[Broker]] will both be notified when a new BLOR is required.
