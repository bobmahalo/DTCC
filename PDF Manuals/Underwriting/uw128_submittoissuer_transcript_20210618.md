electronic certificate is sent to an [[issuer]] for e-signing from the [[Underwriting]] Central (UWC) application as it was

included in the Notes panel of the eLearning player. This text and the annotations on the slides themselves

appearing in the course outline. These are included to help mitigate any confusion which may result from 
referencing page numbers in this transcript. We encourage you to review this and other learning resources 
about [[Underwriting]] Central available on the Learning Center and to complete the “Submit to [[issuer]]: Step 5 of 
the CD Creation Wizard” online survey to provide us with your feedback.

1.2 STEP 5 OF THE CD CREATION WIZARD 
 
Notes: 
For a security to close, the CD created as part of an offering must be sent to the [[issuer]] for e-signing. 
Step 5 of the CD Creation Wizard, the last step in the submission process, is when electronic certificates get 
sent to an [[issuer]] for signature.

1.3 Ready for Submission 7 
1.3 READY FOR SUBMISSION 
 
Notes: 
Your ability to send certificates depends upon their status. 
After being submitted to [[DTC]] in step 4 of the wizard, a certificate is assigned the status of Pending [[DTC]] 
Review. The offering must satisfy prerequisites to meet [[DTC]] approval. Once all of these prerequisites are met, 
the security will be assigned the status of Ready For Submission.  
For more information about security prerequisites, see the [[Underwriting]] Central (UWC) Quick Guide.

1.11 Important Certificate Statuses 15 
1.11 IMPORTANT CERTIFICATE STATUSES 
 
Notes: 
As mentioned during this eLearning, a status of Ready for Submission means that the certificate has passed 
all of [[DTC]]’s checks and may now be sent to the [[issuer]], and a status of Pending [[issuer]] Signature means that 
the certificate was successfully sent to the [[issuer]] who must take the next action. 
If after submitting a certificate you see an Undeliverable status, it means that the certificate was not sent 
successfully, probably because an email address is incorrect. You’ll need to contact your assigned [[DTC]] 
specialist to void the certificate. You can then correct the email address before attempting to resend. 
If the status is Declined, then an [[issuer]] has received and rejected the certificate. You’ll need to contact the 
[[issuer]] to find out why it was declined before modifying the security and resubmitting. 
A Signed status will appear once an [[issuer]] has received and digitally signed the certificate.
