Dashboards 9 
1. DASHBOARDS 
This chapter explains the concept of dashboards, and describes how to use the dashboard to complete tasks 
within the scheduled timeframe. 
Concept of [[Underwriting]] Dashboards 
UW SOURCE provides a home page called ‘Dashboard’  that acts as the central hub for you to communicate 
with The [[Depository Trust Company]] ([[DTC]]) and perform tasks in UW SOURCE. At any point, the dashboard 
displays the work items that require your intervention. In addition, the dashboard enables you to quickly access 
specific screens and queries, and serves as a reference point for interacting with UW SOURCE. 
The dashboard interface differs based on the login details of the user. That is, the dashboard interface is different 
for [[DTC]] staff and external users such as participants and correspondents. 
Types of [[Underwriting]] Dashboards 
UW SOURCE supports the following two types of dashboards:  
• Internal dashboards are meant for the [[DTC]] staff. All the work items generated during the processing 
of a deal and that require intervention from the [[DTC]] staff are listed here.  
• External dashboards  are meant for underwriters, agents, and crediting participants. The external 
dashboard has hyperlinks for the individual [[Asset]] classes that a user can access. However, access 
privileges for the external users to these links are based on the external user entitlements.  Therefore, 
you have links to [[Equity]], Corporate Debt, Municipal Debt, [[ABS]]/CMO, CDs, and All. However, ‘All’ shows 
only those [[Asset]] classes that you can access.

Using Dashboard Queries 
Underwriters and agents have a set of queries on their dashboard. Correspondents can view only the offers that 
they have entered. Participants can view only the offers for which they are either an [[underwriter]] or crediting 
[[participant]]. Against each query, UW SOURCE displays the number of items (Number of Issues/ [[securities]] as 
applicable) indicating the output of that query. To view the query results, click a query and navigate.  
The output of the dashboard queries is based on the following external issue types: 
• [[Equity]] 
• Corporate Debt 
• Municipal Debt 
• [[Asset]] Backed [[securities]] / Collateralized Mortgage Obligation ([[ABS]]/ CMO ) 
• Certificate of Deposits (CDs) 
• All 
 
Figure 2: Dashboard Queries 
To view the set of queries corresponding to an issue type, click the issue type from the external issue type list.  
Note: When you navigate to the Home page (Dashboard), UW SOURCE may not calculate the query counts in 
real-time due to performance reasons. To recalculate the query, click the Refresh icon located to the left of the 
query.

GLOSSARY 
Certificate  
A [[Stock]] certificate (also known as certificate of [[Stock]] or share certificate) is a legal document that certifies 
ownership of a specific number of [[Stock]] [[shares]] (or fractions thereof) in a corporation. 
Correspondent [[underwriter]]  
An [[underwriter]] that utilizes the services of a clearing firm to settle the transaction through [[DTC]]. The 
correspondent [[underwriter]] is not a [[Member]] of [[DTC]]. 
[[CUSIP]]  
The identification number created by the American Banking Associations Committee on Uniform Security 
Identification Procedures ([[CUSIP]]) to uniquely identify issuers and issues of [[securities]] and financial 
instruments. The [[CUSIP]] number consists of nine digits; the first six digits identify the [[issuer]] and have been 
assigned to issuers in alphabetic sequence, and the next two characters (alphabetic or numeric) identify the 
issue. The ninth digit is the check digit. 
Dissemination [[underwriter]]  
The Lead or managing [[underwriter]] with responsibility to submit new issue information for dissemination. The 
dissemination agent will generally be the Lead Manager, however, in cases where there are joint lead 
managers, the managers must decide which firm will submit to [[DTC]] for regulatory purposes. 
[[DTC]]  
Subsidiary of the Depository Trust and Clearing Corporation ([[DTCC]]).  It handles the [[settlement]] of trades in 
corporate and municipal [[securities]] and provides [[custody]] and [[Asset]] servicing for more than two million 
[[securities]] issues from the United States and 100 other countries and territories. 
Final Offering Document  
The final version of an offering document issued for corporations or municipalities.  It contains the complete 
and final information about the offering. 
Issue  
Stocks or [[Bonds]] sold by a corporation, municipality, or government entity. 
[[issuer]]  
A legal entity that develops and sells [[securities]] to finance its operations. 
Offering  
An offering is when a corporation, municipality or government entity elects to bring a new issue to the market in 
order to raise capital. 
Offering Activity  
The information about an offering that is received from underwriters, agents, issuers, [[issuer]]’s counsel, service 
providers, and [[DTC]].
