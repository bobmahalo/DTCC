UW SOURCE – 871(M) ATTESTATION QUICK TIP 
VERSION 1 
MAY 22, 2017 
Did you know? 
Section 871(m) of the Internal Revenue Code effectively imposes withholding tax on foreign investors holding 
[[Equity]]-linked instruments when there is a [[Dividend]] equivalent payment even in the absence of cash payments.  
[[Equity]] linked instruments that have a “delta” of 0.80 or greater, as calculated by the [[issuer]] at the time of 
issuance, will be subject to 871(m) withholdings. Additionally, issuers will need to distinguish whether the 
contract (i.e. security) is a “simple contract” or a “complex contract.” Underwriters will need to attest to whether 
[[securities]] are considered “section 871(m) transactions” when submitting [[DTC]] eligibility requests. 
[[DTC]] provides information-only announcements regarding the timing and the amount of [[Dividend]] equivalent 
payments on 871(m) transactions received from issuers and maintains a record of which [[securities]] are 
considered in-scope for section 871(m). The UW Source system supports this process by identifying 871(m) 
transactions and gathering the required information from underwriters at the point of issue eligibility.

This Quick Tip shows underwriters how to make the necessary 871(m) attestations when submitting an issue 
for [[DTC]] eligibility in the UW Source system. 
ABOUT 871 (M) ATTESTATIONS  
To support compliance with Internal Revenue Code section 871(m), underwriters submitting [[DTC]] eligibility 
requests must attest on behalf and as agent of the [[issuer]], to the following information for new issues directly in 
UW Source.

MAKING 871 (M) ATTESTATIONS  
1. On the UW Source Dashboard, in the Action Required Items area, click the Pending 871(m) 
Attestation link.

The Pending 871(m) Attestation page is displayed where you can make 871(m) attestations on issues and 
submit the information to [[DTC]].  
 
2. Select the issue for which you are making the attestation. You may select multiple or all issues by 
clicking the check boxes in the selection column. 
 
 
 
Note 
If you select multiple issues, the answers to the 871(m) questions should apply to all the issues selected. 
Otherwise, select and submit issue attestations individually.

Dashboard, the number of pending 871(m) items is reduced to reflect the submission. 
Note
